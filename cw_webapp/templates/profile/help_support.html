Frequently Asked Questions

What is CodeWeave?

CodeWeave is a comprehensive project management platform designed specifically for developers and development teams. It helps you organize projects, track progress, collaborate with team members, and manage your development workflow efficiently.

How do I get started with CodeWeave?

Getting started is easy! Simply create an account, set up your profile, and start creating your first project. You can invite team members, set up tasks, and begin tracking your development progress right away.

Is CodeWeave free to use?

CodeWeave offers both free and premium plans. The free plan includes basic features for individual developers, while premium plans offer advanced features for teams and larger organizations.

How do I create a new project?

To create a new project, click the "Create Project" button in your dashboard. Fill in the project details including name, description, and initial settings. You can then add team members and start creating tasks.

Can I invite team members to my projects?

Yes! You can invite team members by going to your project settings and clicking "Invite Members". Enter their email addresses and they'll receive an invitation to join your project.

How do I track project progress?

Project progress is automatically tracked based on completed tasks and milestones. You can view detailed analytics in the project dashboard, including completion rates, time tracking, and team performance.

How do I change my password?

Go to your account settings and click on "Security". From there, you can change your password, enable two-factor authentication, and manage other security settings.

Can I export my project data?

Yes, you can export your project data in various formats including JSON, CSV, and PDF. Go to your project settings and look for the "Export Data" option.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account, go to your account settings and scroll to the bottom. Click on "Delete Account" and follow the confirmation process. Note that this action cannot be undone.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Go to your billing settings and click "Cancel Subscription". You'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions. If you're not satisfied with our service, contact our support team within 30 days of your first payment for a full refund.
